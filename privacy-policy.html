Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026  ·  Legaldirectory.ai  ·  Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

1. Introduction

Legaldirectory.ai ("we", "us", "Platform") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using our Platform, you consent to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and property address provided during booking or enquiry.
  • Document Data: Property documents, identity documents, and legal papers uploaded voluntarily for legal review or service delivery.
  • Payment Data: Transaction identifiers processed via Razorpay. We do not store card numbers or UPI credentials on our servers.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, device type, and browser information collected via Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager.
  • Communication Data: Messages, enquiries, and correspondence sent via WhatsApp or email.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To assign your service request to a suitable empanelled lawyer.
  • To send booking confirmations, service updates, and delivery notifications.
  • To process payments securely through Razorpay.
  • To respond to your queries and provide customer support.
  • To improve Platform performance, user experience, and service quality.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Empanelled Lawyers: Your name, contact, and relevant documents are shared with the assigned lawyer solely to deliver your service.
  • Payment Processors: Transaction data is processed by Razorpay under their own Privacy Policy.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government authority.

5. Document Security

Property documents and legal papers shared with us are used solely for the purpose of the service booked. Documents are handled with strict confidentiality and are not retained beyond the service delivery period, unless you have explicitly opted into our Document Locker service.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies through Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand usage patterns and improve our Platform. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; however, some functionality may be affected. We do not use advertising cookies or sell behavioural data to advertisers.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time (this will not affect processing already completed).

To exercise these rights, write to weightage.lab@gmail.com.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data for a period of 12 months from the date of last service interaction, after which it is securely deleted unless retention is required by applicable law.

9. Third-Party Links

Our Platform may contain links to third-party websites such as Razorpay or WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these websites and encourage you to review their policies separately.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Platform is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with data, please contact us immediately.
