Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026  ·  Legaldirectory.ai  ·  Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

1. Our Commitment

Legaldirectory.ai is committed to delivering every service to the highest standard. If you are unsatisfied with a delivered service, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will work to resolve your concern fairly.

2. Eligible Refund Scenarios

A full refund will be issued in the following circumstances:

  • The service was not initiated within the committed timeframe and no prior notice was communicated.
  • A duplicate payment was made by the user for the same service booking.
  • The service was cancelled by Legaldirectory.ai due to inability to assign a suitable lawyer.
  • A verified technical error resulted in an incorrect charge.
  • The user cancels within 2 hours of payment and before lawyer assignment.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • The service has been delivered in full — report, drafted document, or government document procured.
  • The refund request is raised more than 7 days after service delivery.
  • Cancellation is requested after a lawyer has been assigned and work has commenced.
  • Delays were caused by the user's failure to provide required documents or information in a timely manner.
  • Dissatisfaction with the legal outcome (property title decision, court ruling, government rejection) which is beyond the Platform's control.
  • Change of mind after service delivery.

4. Partial Refunds

In cases where work has partially commenced, a partial refund may be considered at Legaldirectory.ai's sole discretion, based on the extent of work completed. This will be communicated in writing.

5. Refund Process & Timelines

  • Approved refunds will be credited to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking, wallet).
  • Processing time: 7–10 working days from approval date.
  • Razorpay and bank processing timelines may vary and are outside our control.
  • Refund confirmation will be sent via WhatsApp and email.

6. Liaising Officer (₹99 Service)

For the Liaising Officer document procurement service, refunds are available only if the document was not procured within 48 hours with no prior communication from our team. Once a document has been successfully procured and delivered, no refund is applicable.
