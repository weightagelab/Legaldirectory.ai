Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026  ·  Legaldirectory.ai  ·  Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

1. Cancellation by User — Before Lawyer Assignment

If you cancel within 2 hours of payment and before a lawyer has been assigned, you are entitled to a full refund. To cancel, contact us immediately via WhatsApp or email with your booking reference.

2. Cancellation by User — After Lawyer Assignment

Once a lawyer has been assigned to your booking:

  • Before work commencement: Up to 50% of the service fee may be retained as assignment and coordination charges.
  • After work has commenced: No refund will be issued. Partial credit may be offered at the Platform's discretion based on work completed.

"Work commencement" is defined as the lawyer having reviewed documents, initiated a client call, commenced drafting, or taken any substantive action on your service request.

3. How to Cancel

Contact us immediately via one of the following channels. Include your name, service booked, and payment reference number:

  • WhatsApp: +91 90089 99968 (fastest response)
  • Email: weightage.lab@gmail.com

Cancellation requests are processed during business hours: Monday–Sunday, 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM IST.

4. Cancellation by Legaldirectory.ai

We reserve the right to cancel a booking in the following circumstances, with a full refund:

  • No suitable empanelled lawyer is available in your area or for your service type.
  • The user fails to provide required documents within 48 hours of booking without communication.
  • The request falls outside our current scope of operations or geographic coverage.
  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, government restrictions, etc.).

5. Rescheduling

Rescheduling of lawyer home visits or consultation slots is permitted up to 2 hours before the scheduled time at no charge. Late rescheduling or no-shows may result in a rescheduling fee of up to ₹500 or forfeiture of the slot.

6. Liaising Officer (₹99 Service)

Cancellations for the Liaising Officer document procurement service must be made within 1 hour of booking. After that, the fee is non-refundable as document procurement may have already commenced with the relevant government portal or office.

7. Refund After Cancellation

Approved refunds following cancellation will be processed to the original payment method within 7–10 working days. See our Refund Policy for full details.
